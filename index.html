Our Story

Rooted in Precision and Consistency, Powered by Innovation

Established on 50 hectares of fertile land between Minna and Bida in Nigeria, we operate the largest commercial crop farm in the region. Our mission is simple: grow better food using smarter systems.

  • Precision Drip Irrigation

    Every drop counts. We nourish cops with water saving root-targeted systems

  • Sensor-based monitoring

    Real time insights from soil to sky, ensuring optimal crop health

  • GPS-guided machinery

    Smart Equipment that maximizes efficiency and reduces labor costs

  • Data-driven crop decisions

    From planting to harvest, We let data guide every move

Modern Farm Equipment

Agricultural Tractor for Land Preparation at Shebe Farms

At Shebe Farms, our tractors are used for efficient soil tillage and land preparation, turning and loosening soil to improve fertility and ensure optimal conditions for crop planting.


2.

Field Cultivator for Soil Leveling at Shebe Farms

Our modern cultivator equipment breaks up soil clumps and levels farmland, helping Shebe Farms create well-prepared seedbeds that support uniform crop growth and higher yields.



3.

Precision Crop Sprayer at Shebe

Shebe Farms uses advanced agricultural sprayers to apply fertilizers and crop protection solutions evenly, promoting healthy plants while improving farm productivity and sustainability


4.

Mechanical Seed Planter at Shebe Farms

The precision planting machines at Shebe Farms ensure accurate seed spacing and depth, increasing planting efficiency and supporting consistent, high-quality planting.
